# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-30

Cubby is a file manager for macOS. This Privacy Policy explains how Cubby handles information.

## Information collection

Cubby does not require an account or sign-in and does not collect personal information.

The app stores its settings, sidebar, remembered folder views, and open windows as files in `~/.config/cubby` on your Mac. This information is not sent to the developer or any third party.

## Analytics and advertising

Cubby does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

## Data sharing

Cubby does not sell, rent, or share personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

Your settings remain on your Mac until you delete `~/.config/cubby` or uninstall the app with `brew uninstall --zap cubby`.

## Children's privacy

Cubby is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated policy will be posted with a revised effective date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, open an issue at https://github.com/grahamotte/Cubby/issues.
